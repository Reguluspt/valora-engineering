## 2. D1 — Business meaning and COMPLETE

The accepted professional checkpoint is **one explicit human confirmation of the entire prepared exact sealed-line set**, producing one current valid A13 quotation-item selection per line. Scope of durable history/head remains organization + Project + exact line; scope of the positive command/receipt is the whole Project selection set. Selection records the reviewed offer for later comparison/presentation. Supplier-global state, quotation confirmation and final appraisal price are separate authorities. Selecting the same Supplier on many lines is allowed, with a distinct eligible item for each exact line.

Let `M` be the exact non-empty current sealed set inherited from SUPPLIER_QUOTES. Let `H(l)` be the uniquely resolved latest selection revision for exact line `l`, and `Eligible(item, snapshot, now)` be current A13 authority, not a duplicated predicate. Recommend:

```text
future selection capability separately accepted, implemented and certified
AND current SUPPLIER_QUOTES = COMPLETE, including its upstream closure
AND tenant/Project/Intake/seal/membership correspondence exact and non-empty
AND no applicable accepted Blocking concern or unprovable required integrity
AND for every l in M:
    exactly one H(l), kind confirmed-selection rather than withdrawal
    AND intact immutable fact + command receipt + atomic audit
    AND authenticated human whole-set command/receipt lineage proves this line's selection
    AND its original exact-set manifest and per-line effect are intact
    AND exact selected item is Eligible for this tenant/Project/l now
    AND selection dependency bindings are current with no retained invalidation
```

This is compute-on-read from authoritative facts. No persisted stage-done Boolean, page visit, radio state, Supplier ID, legacy `selected` label or head pointer alone establishes COMPLETE. An empty set does not complete vacuously. Head uniqueness without valid lineage does not count.

The sole positive commit is **“Xác nhận danh sách NCC đã chọn”**. It directly attests the exact reviewed manifest and creates required per-line facts atomically; it is not an extra stage-done fact layered over per-line clicks. Later whole-set commits may retain unchanged current heads and append changed/reconfirmed successors, so heads can reference different historical receipts; each must have intact original whole-set lineage and remain valid now. Prepopulation, local overrides and a fully prepared table are not official facts and cannot satisfy COMPLETE. No per-line confirmation, additional modal/checklist, mandatory warning acknowledgement, second approval or re-attestation follows a successful set commit. Transactional details remain D1/D4/D9 recommendations PENDING; the confirmation granularity is already ACCEPTED.

## 4. D3 — Eligible candidates and exact A13 binding

Recommend using the **same coherent A13 authority fold** used by SUPPLIER_QUOTES. Do not assume `SupplierQuoteItem.fact_id` identifies the confirmation or the quote revision. It identifies the fact that wrote that item: registration/revision or line-registration. Persist and verify the tuple:

```text

+ effective SupplierQuoteItem ID and its owning fact_id
```

### Accepted editable suggestion; pending deterministic ranking contract

For a line without a current official selection, the server automatically proposes the lowest Decimal quoted unit price only when a safe common comparison basis is proved across the eligible offers. Reuse exact A13 item/quantity/unit/currency and `same_working_unit_basis` authority; require identical currency, unit and exact sealed-line quantity plus compatible declared tax and commercial terms. A13's warning-comparability flag alone does not prove equal tax/terms across suppliers. Its current schema does not establish a normalized cross-offer tax/terms ranking contract; the scoped successor ADR/runtime contract must define the available explicit proof and conservative comparison policy before runtime. Unknown or differing currency/unit/quantity/tax/terms makes a global minimum unprovable. Do not invent FX, unit, quantity, tax, discount, delivery or payment-term conversion, normalize missing declarations into equality, infer comparability from equal numbers, or silently choose a minimum within one of several incomparable groups. A missing working price does not itself preclude ranking offers whose common basis is otherwise proved; the working-price warning remains independently unavailable.

Recommend deterministic tie policy: find the exact Decimal minimum; expose **every equally-low eligible item**, the tie count and stable tie order, and prepopulate the lexicographically smallest canonical `(Supplier ID, quote_id, revision fact ID, item ID)` tuple among them. Canonical UUID text order, candidate-set digest and ranking-policy version are server-owned and recorded in preparation/provenance. The tie-break conveys no supplier preference or price advantage; ties require no extra confirmation click. The precise algorithm/version remains PENDING. Candidate order, pagination, refresh timing and browser number arithmetic cannot pick the winner.

Show **“Không xác định được giá thấp nhất trên cùng cơ sở so sánh”** and specific safe reasons when ranking cannot be proved. Leave that line's new choice unset until the professional deliberately selects an eligible exact-line offer; this choice resolves the exception within preparation, with no separate acknowledgement/attestation. Confirming the whole set requires a deliberate choice for every such line. One eligible offer can be proposed only when its comparison basis is safe; no mandatory-three rule or arbitrary minimum quote count is introduced. The professional can override any default with another eligible exact-line item, including an unrankable eligible offer after deliberate choice, with honest comparison status.

Preparation distinguishes conceptual `SYSTEM_SUGGESTED`, `USER_OVERRIDDEN`/deliberate exception choice and `OFFICIALLY_CONFIRMED`; these are proposed display/provenance concepts, not new public enums. An existing current official selection remains the prepared choice on refresh, even if another offer is now cheaper. Show the fresh cheapest suggestion separately; only deliberate override plus the whole-set human commit changes the official choice. For stale heads, show old history separately from fresh proposed replacements; one whole-set commit can explicitly reconfirm them. Neither a refresh nor a ranking result writes professional facts.

### Durable invalidation authority — recommended D5/D8/D9 decision

Recommend an append-only **`SupplierSelectionInvalidationObservation`** fact, distinct from the human selection journal and from Case State. This is a technical observation of lost authority, never a persisted stage-status flag. Its immutable facts are: observation UUID; organization/Project/exact line; exact target selection fact ID/revision/head expectation; monotonically increasing per-line `invalidation_generation`; dependency kind/identity, bounded protected observation proof (status/availability/verification result/generation/checksum/size as applicable) and canonical before/observed proof digests; bounded reason code; trusted observation UTC time; server observer identity/kind; command UUID/contract/request digest/receipt/audit IDs; expected shared Case State token and pre/post Project versions. The deduplication key is organization + Project + line + target selection fact + dependency fingerprint + reason code; a replay never allocates another generation. A digest alone without its retained typed observation proof is insufficient.

The proposed sole writer is internal **`RecordSupplierSelectionInvalidation`**, invoked by the trusted server dependency-observation service using an existing **system principal**, scoped to the verified tenant/Project and allowlisted deterministic D8 technical reasons. It has no public client API, new RBAC grant, AI judgment, freeform client-supplied verdict or power to confirm/select/rebind/withdraw. Source observations use the existing bounded retained-byte verifier; database dependencies use the authoritative A13/upstream resolver. A human selection command may request this same technical writer when revalidation discovers a fault, but cannot fabricate its proof. No other writer may insert or clear observations directly.

Trigger this writer when a current positive selection's dependency is proved non-current by a participating dependency mutation or trusted refresh/verification, including a transient source-availability/integrity fault. The **provider/projector remains read-only**. Application read orchestration must perform trusted dependency observation through this internal command boundary before publishing a refreshed positive projection: if it detects a new fault, first durably record the observation, then project STALE. A fault must not be published as a known stale observation and subsequently forgotten. If observation persistence/audit is unavailable, fail closed with UNAVAILABLE/error and publish no current/COMPLETE assertion. This narrowly specified technical observation side effect is a proposed change to read orchestration; it is not a hidden selection commit or a pure GET/provider database write. No asynchronous best-effort event, process-local latch, TTL or hash-only CAS may substitute for the durable ordering barrier.

The writer locks **Project first**, then the same deterministic dependency/line path and exact selection head. Require exact tenant/Project/line/target head, expected Project/shared Case State/selection revision and previously read per-line invalidation generation. Allocate `generation + 1` under that Project lock; append fact, receipt and minimized `NCC_SELECTION_MARKED_STALE` audit, increment Project version once, and commit atomically. If a competing successor already moved the head, reject the old target with CAS conflict and observe the new head afresh; never attach an old fault to the successor. A trusted fault captured while the expected head was current remains a valid historical observation even if bytes recover before its diagnostic commit. Revalidate the scope/head and captured proof provenance, not a requirement that the transient fault still be present, which would lose the event. Duplicate UUID/fingerprint returns its existing historical receipt. Audit failure rolls back fact/generation/receipt/Project together and prevents a positive refreshed read. If a failed professional command discovers the fault, first roll back that command completely, then record the observation in its own bounded internal transaction against the still-current head; do not discard the fault by nesting its diagnostic inside a transaction that will roll back. Failed selection still produces zero selection success audits/receipts.

If the shared token/Project version changes solely through recovery or another dependency/line command while the same exact target head remains current, a definite no-write CAS conflict permits the technical observer to refresh invocation versions and resubmit the **same retained captured fault**, using a new command UUID for the changed invocation and the same observation identity/deduplication key. Do not replace that fault with a now-healthy observation or drop it. Unknown diagnostic outcomes reconcile the original receipt first. This technical retry grants no professional-command auto-retry and no authority to move or re-attest a selection head.

This technical writer may record invalidation in DRAFT or non-DRAFT because currentness remains observable outside DRAFT; it **cannot** change selection head, chosen item, human confirmation, quotation/upstream facts or final price. All professional selection writes retain D6's DRAFT requirement. Observation/receipt reads require current authorized project scope. No public permission to invoke a system observation is added. A corrupt/unprovable observation journal itself fails closed and cannot be waived by a successor.

For a positive head, any intact retained invalidation targeting **that exact selection revision** permanently makes it non-current, even after A13 eligibility/source availability returns. A13's current eligibility and the shared content-hash token cannot clear that fact. Only an explicit human whole-set commit with a reconfirm/change row successor, binding the exact predecessor and latest invalidation generation in its CAS, establishes a new positive revision with freshly eligible dependencies. It acknowledges retained observations in its immutable predecessor context; old observations remain immutable and do not automatically attach to the new revision. Withdrawal instead establishes a tombstone, with no positive selection. Read-derived COMPLETE requires both current A13 eligibility and zero invalidations targeting the current positive head. Generations advance only for new durable observations; they are not time ticks or a second Case State algorithm.

This is a **PENDING business-visible D5/D8/D9 design recommendation**, including the technical observation command and persistence requirement. PO Option A does not accept the coordinator, sticky invalidation or diagnostic transaction architecture. A later accepted scoped ADR/runtime contract must settle physical fields, orchestration, performance and proofs; A15 implements none of it. ### Observation coordinator, transaction scope and CAS liveness

Recommend a new **`SupplierSelectionObservationCoordinator` application service**, outside `resolve_authority`, every provider and `get_case_state_projection`'s pure projection. **Read path:** authenticate existing `project:read`/tenant access; obtain a coherent read-only snapshot and trusted dependency observations; healthy reads with no new invalidation perform **no diagnostic write or Project write lock**. For new proved faults on current positive heads, close the read transaction, retain their typed proofs, then issue separately scoped `RecordSupplierSelectionInvalidation` transactions in stable sealed-line/dependency order. After a successful full-set commit, separately record only faults still applicable to the exact resulting heads with freshly read CAS. Do not nest diagnostic commits inside another command's outer transaction or let an observation's Project increment invalidate that command's final-commit self-check. One whole-set command receipt, every affected revision/head and all success audits remain one atomic professional outcome. A later diagnostic failure cannot retroactively roll back an already committed selection or erase its receipt; return/reconcile that truthful historical receipt and expose current Case State as unverified/UNAVAILABLE until coordinator refresh succeeds, with further writes locked. Do not report current COMPLETE or retry the professional command because follow-up diagnosis failed. Separate command/audit identities distinguish successful technical observations from selection success audits after a rejected selection.

A previously prepared whole-set professional command may therefore receive 409; this is the existing shared-Project CAS policy, not an invisible read-only token change or a new per-line token. Current preparation/read responses use the post-diagnostic token so they do not immediately conflict with their own observation. ## 8. D7 — One canonical server warning calculation

Recommend **reuse A13's `comparison_warnings` through one shared canonical server comparison function**, resolving the current working reference after locks at selection commit. Preparation, including unselected lines, uses the live shared function against the coherent current candidate/reference. Commit persists its result/snapshot/calculation identity; history displays that immutable snapshot. Current-selection presentation distinguishes the commit snapshot from any live comparison and reconciles reference changes under D8; it never pretends unselected preparation has a stored selection snapshot. Do not reuse legacy `_compute_warnings`, which rounds percent before threshold comparison, or calculate thresholds in React. Future extraction of a shared function must keep A13's accepted behavior and tests intact.

For comparable current working price `C > 0` and quoted Decimal price `S`:

```text
difference_amount = S - C
difference_percent = (S - C) / C * 100
below_working_price when S < C
difference_exceeds_15_percent when abs(S - C) * 100 > C * 15
```

Exactly 15% is not the difference warning. Below-working-price still warns at any smaller deviation; both codes survive when both apply. They are **WARNING, never Blocking**, and cannot disable a server-permitted commit or create another checkpoint.

Comparability follows A13's explicit `same_working_unit_basis` declaration and exact quantity/unit/currency compatibility; no currency/unit conversion is inferred. Missing working price or incomparable basis: both differences NULL and no percentage/threshold assertion. Comparable zero working price may retain arithmetic difference `S`, but percent is NULL and no percentage/below-positive-reference warning is fabricated; zero remains distinct from missing. Preparation/history returns the reason when comparison is unavailable. Optional acknowledgements are a validated subset of canonical visible codes; they cannot remove warning truth or become a required completion fact.
