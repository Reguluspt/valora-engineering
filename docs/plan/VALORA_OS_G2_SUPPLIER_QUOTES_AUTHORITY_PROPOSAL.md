## 2. D1–D3: meaning, scope and minimum coverage

The professional attests: **“This identified supplier has confirmed these quoted items/terms in this retained source, and I have checked the exact quotation revision against these prepared assets.”** PRICE_EVIDENCE attests the basis for considering price; SUPPLIER_QUOTES attests supplier-specific offers; SUPPLIER_SELECTION chooses an offer; APPRAISAL_RESULT records the later final professional result. Proposed/offered prices pending supplier confirmation are not confirmed prices.

Let `M` be the exact non-empty sealed Project asset set inherited from current PRICE_EVIDENCE. Let `Q(l)` be confirmed quotation lines for exact line `l` whose supplier, source, facts, confirmation and dependency closure are current and intact. Proposed COMPLETE is exactly:

```text
accepted and certified future SUPPLIER_QUOTES capability exists
AND current PRICE_EVIDENCE = COMPLETE (including its upstream dependencies)
AND tenant / Project / seal / membership correspondence is exact and non-empty
AND no applicable accepted Blocking issue or unprovable required proof
AND for every l in M: distinct resolved independent suppliers in Q(l) >= 1
AND every counted line belongs to an immutable, explicitly human-confirmed quote revision
AND every counted revision has intact receipt/audit/source/lineage and is effective and unexpired
```

This is a read-derived predicate, never a persisted stage-done Boolean. Page visits, local state, checkboxes, selected rows, drafts, uploads, text supplier names, legacy ACTIVE or an amount alone cannot satisfy it. No project-wide quote-set confirmation is recommended: individual supplier documents are the business attestation; an additional set-wide button would repeat coverage without adding an independent business decision.

**D2:** all sealed lines require coverage. No NOT_APPLICABLE state or zero-quote exemption is recommended, and no actor is granted power to declare one. An out-of-scope asset remains unfinished until a separately authorized membership lifecycle resolves it; quote work never adds/removes/replaces members or re-Applies intake. One supplier document may cover many sealed lines; each counted source item has its own exact immutable project-line relationship, item locator and values. Never fan out canonical-asset matches to every similar line or infer mappings from visible grid rows. One source item maps to one exact ProjectAssetLine; repeated identical assets require explicit separate quoted items/allocated quantities supported by the document. No double allocation of an item's quantity. A revision has at least one mapped item; it need not cover the whole Project.

**D3:** minimum **one** current confirmed quotation from one resolved independent supplier per sealed line. Collecting additional independent quotations is encouraged as professional comparison, with no hidden target/three-quote blocker. Multiple offers/documents/revisions from the same legal supplier count once per line; aliases, supplier duplicates or common legal identity are not independence. Unresolved duplicate/merge identity cannot count. Same artifact/item registered twice contributes once, never two suppliers; conflicting attribution of one document is an integrity blocker. Only the current confirmed head of a logical quote contributes. Separate genuine offers from one supplier may remain current but still count that supplier once.

| Option | Analysis / recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| One current confirmed independent supplier per line | **Recommend.** Smallest durable selectable coverage consistent with retained stage separation; permits specialist assets and preserves truth. It is not a claim that one offer meets every external procurement/valuation policy. No external legal rule is invented here. |
| Three independent suppliers per line | More comparison breadth, but may block scarce-market assets and invites duplicate padding/waivers. Company NCC1/NCC2/NCC3 columns are presentation, not minimum authority. Do not infer this rule. |
| Configurable minimum | Useful only after PO accepts a versioned policy, issuer, scope and exception governance. Hidden per-project/operator settings make identical completion labels mean different facts; reject for initial stage. |
| Risk/category-specific or exception-based minimum | Could reflect specialist markets, but requires accepted categories, responsible authority and truthful downstream handling. More policy than this bounded stage needs; reject initially. |

Insufficient market with one genuine eligible offer satisfies the recommended minimum without waiver; below one is INCOMPLETE. No-market yields zero and cannot complete; do not create `RecordInsufficientSupplierMarketException`. Keep a bounded professional note in quotation work if useful, but it grants no coverage. Withdrawn/rejected/unconfirmed/future-effective quotations count zero; expired or invalidated previously confirmed offers count zero with currentness diagnostics. Never delete history to hide missing coverage. Additional unfinished optional draft offers do not prevent completion already supported by current confirmed coverage.

## 3. D4–D6: quotation identity, lifecycle and human confirmation

It matches one supplier's issued/signed document and the retained child flow “Hoàn tất một báo giá của một NCC”; terms/source/revisions can be checked together. Line-local identity is simpler per grid row but duplicates documents, terms and confirmation, and allows incompatible fragments to appear current. This is a conceptual aggregate, not a table/API design or blind adoption of QuoteBatch.

The professional explicitly checks the supplier's confirmation, not just their own data entry. Signed/stamped source and evidence of the supplier-confirmed prices/terms must support all included items. A proposed quote, unsigned draft, historic price, or locally typed amount stays candidate even if a professional presses a button. Human checking does not claim cryptographic signature verification or external legal authenticity certification. Suspected forged/contradictory source is ineligible until resolved through an authorized process. A12 introduces no outbound RFQ, supplier portal, digital signing or document generation.

**D5 lifecycle:** editable draft → candidate ready for professional review → immutable confirmed revision; currentness is derived. Candidate is a frozen review snapshot; editing creates a new draft generation. Draft modifications use CAS and leave audit/receipt history but confer no coverage. Confirmation freezes the entire supplier document's complete mapped item set atomically; no partial confirmation of a document with unresolved material items. Unmapped external items may be retained as source context but are explicitly excluded from Project coverage and attestation, never silently imported.

Correction, currency/terms/item-map change, source replacement or supplier correction creates an explicit successor draft. Draft successor alone is not confirmed authority and does not silently supersede a still-valid predecessor; however a known error, source replacement or withdrawn offer must immediately be recorded as withdrawal/hold through an authorized command before treating the predecessor as current. `ReviseSupplierQuote` with correction/replacement reason atomically withdraws the incorrect prior confirmed revision and opens a draft successor; ordinary negotiation of an additional successor offer may retain the unchanged valid predecessor. The command explicitly distinguishes these reasons. Once a successor is confirmed, the prior confirmed head is atomically superseded for the entire document; omitted items lose its coverage. Retain all original facts and decisions. Same-state new UUID confirmation is denied; no second confirmation of an already confirmed immutable revision. Reassessment after upstream content/proof change uses a successor revision with a fresh human attestation, even when the supplier document bytes are unchanged.

Withdrawal appends a reasoned reversal and removes current contribution; no automatic fallback to an older revision. Rejection appends a decision on a draft/candidate; it cannot overwrite an existing confirmation or reject a supplier globally. Withdrawal of confirmed authority is distinct from rejection of an unconfirmed proposal. Known authenticity/identity/integrity concerns are unresolved holds, not ordinary benign rejection; they survive successor registration/withdrawal until explicitly resolved by a reasoned checked successor confirmation with evidence. Rejected history does not permanently block unrelated valid offers. Expiry is derived at UTC cutoff without a scheduled status writer. Re-activation is a new successor + explicit confirmation; never delete a reversal or edit dates on an official row.

**D6 “Hoàn tất từng báo giá NCC”** is explicit authenticated human professional confirmation of that exact supplier, immutable quote revision, source artifact and locators, all mapped items and quoted values/currency/unit, material terms, dates/deadlines, supplier-confirmation evidence and provenance limitations. Show this attestation before submission; no implicit confirmation by save/upload/navigation. Existing active human/organization + `workbench:edit`, owned active Workbench session, same-tenant Project DRAFT and the complete §5 envelope are required for every new official quotation command, including withdrawals. Read access uses scoped `project:read`; acquiring a session retains `workbench:open`. No new role, permission or grant, and no inferred `knowledge:approve` substitute. Registrar may also confirm: no four-eyes segregation is recommended for this initial stage. If PO later requires independent approval, that is a separate accepted permission/process decision before runtime; do not grant it silently.

AI/system/worker may never confirm supplier identity, quoted professional facts, source authenticity or completion, nor impersonate the human actor. They may only provide explicitly labeled candidates through separately authorized tooling. Non-DRAFT current completion may remain readable; all new edits/confirmations/reversals are closed. Security incidents outside DRAFT use existing authorized access/incident controls and separate owner-approved recovery, not a hidden quote mutation bypass. No return-to-DRAFT command is proposed.

## 4. D7–D9: price boundary, currentness, warnings and blockers

**D7:** a quote may pin exact immutable PRICE_EVIDENCE source/relationship/confirmation IDs as contextual lineage, after scoped access checks, but must independently retain supplier-issued facts and supplier confirmation. Context is explicitly non-qualifying quotation provenance by itself. No automatic PRICE_EVIDENCE → QuoteLine or QuoteLine → qualifying PRICE_EVIDENCE conversion; no silent copying of amounts, acceptance or supplier identity. Quote work neither writes/withdraws PRICE_EVIDENCE nor resets its confirmation. Internet → explanation → historical appraisal-result priority remains unchanged. Existing optional `appraised_unit_price` stays comparative working data under its existing Human Commit command, not a quote requirement or final price. An independent working-price change may stale upstream authority under A9/A10; quotes must respect that consequence without changing those semantics.

**D8:** use canonical content/proof digests, exact identities and receipt/audit integrity, not row versions alone, to derive business currentness. A quote command's own Project increment cannot stale its newly committed facts. Revisions are immutable; apply currentness to current confirmed heads and their dependency closure, not every abandoned historical record.

| Change / condition | Proposed effect and recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Proven seal/membership/tenant/project-line divergence | BLOCKED; corrupt/unprovable referenced correspondence STALE. No membership repair, relabeling or inferred ownership. |
| Actual description/specification/approved identity/quantity/unit changes; upstream review/validation/Workbench or PRICE_EVIDENCE confirmation generation change | Affected confirmed item bindings non-current. Preserve upstream stage/result and restore upstream first; then explicitly register/confirm checked successor quote against new facts. Never carry forward confirmation by matching text. |
| PRICE_EVIDENCE no longer current COMPLETE | Quote COMPLETE impossible. Existing quote dependencies become non-current. Expose original earliest unfinished upstream stage/action; do not call ordinary upstream INCOMPLETE a new price-stage STALE. |
| Supplier merge/deactivation or material legal identity correction | Its quotations lose current eligibility; known inactive/unresolved identity is a blocker for that offer. Never follow merge target automatically. New human-confirmed supplier snapshot and supported source identity required; registration cannot alter Supplier master data. Cosmetic display/alias changes alone do not rewrite historical snapshots or necessarily stale material identity. |
| Source artifact lost/unavailable/integrity mismatch or byte replacement | Dependent quotation STALE; explicit known cross-tenant or integrity contradiction BLOCKED. Suppress eligibility, keep history. Restore exact proved immutable artifact or create a reasoned successor; no same-ID replacement. |
| Currency/terms/unit/value/map correction, source or quote supersession | Explicit successor authority only; confirmation binds whole item set. No automatic rebind of quotation or selection. |
| Withdrawal / rejected / unconfirmed offer | No contribution. Benign withdrawal with intact history is ordinary missing coverage INCOMPLETE if no alternate current eligible supplier; known unresolved hold remains BLOCKED. Rejected/unconfirmed optional work alone cannot erase valid coverage. |
| Expiry / review deadline / effective date | Current iff `effective_at <= now < min(review_due_at, supplier_expires_at if supplied)`. Future-effective confirmed history contributes zero; expired counted head STALE. Another independently current quote can still cover the line; an expired optional historical offer does not permanently poison stage completion. |
| Project status / access / session / CAS-only change | Non-DRAFT closes new writes; intact content-current COMPLETE may survive. Unfinished work requiring mutation outside DRAFT is BLOCKED. Revoked access fails closed; no page/session/permission requirement becomes business completion. A stale invocation token conflicts; it does not itself make durable facts STALE. |
| Confirmation withdrawal, broken proof/receipt/audit or unresolved material concern | Withdrawal retained and no contribution/fallback. Missing/corrupt required integrity is STALE; known contradiction/negative hold BLOCKED until explicit authorized resolution. A corrupt predecessor cannot be waived by creating a successor. |

Staleness of an additional optional quote does not by itself invalidate complete independent coverage. Active unresolved integrity/identity/authenticity holds on the Project/line remain blockers and cannot be hidden by adding another quote. Assess the exact admitted current closure; historical superseded/withdrawn offers are inspectable, not current contributors. A historical receipt is never evidence that its offer remains current.

**Deadline at transaction commit:** resolve one coherent UTC observation for reads, and recheck effective/expiry/review cutoffs after lock waits and at the outer transaction's final commit boundary, using current server time, not transaction-start time. No long-running caller work after the final recheck. If cutoff is crossed before that boundary, roll back fact/head/receipt/audit together; no “confirmed before expiry” backdating. A final commit-time recheck defines the authority's linearization point; immediate expiry afterward suppresses completion on the next read even if the receipt records a valid historical commit. Later implementation must prove both sides of the boundary with a deterministic clock and real PostgreSQL locks. Hash deterministic freshness/deadline state in the one shared CAS, never wall-clock ticks or a second parallel token algorithm.

**D9 Decimal comparison:** `S` is the source-confirmed Decimal unit price, `C` the current optional working unit price, with identical resolved currency, exact quantity/unit, tax, delivery and condition basis. Compare unrounded exact values; display rounding must not change warning thresholds. `delta = S - C`; `S < C` always warns; for positive `C`, `abs(S-C)*100 > C*15` warns (equivalent to absolute percentage >15%, strict boundary). Signed display percent is `(S-C)/C*100`; do not use binary float or rounded displayed percent for classification. If `C=NULL`, differences/percent are null and comparison is unavailable; no missing-price blocker. For `C=0`, percentage is null; absolute delta may be shown on a comparable basis. Nonpositive/unusable working basis or incompatible currency/unit/tax/terms produces a safe non-comparable diagnostic, no invented FX or percentage. A material difference in offer terms is retained for professional review, not auto-normalized.

Examples on identical basis: `C=100,S=99` warns below; `S=85` warns below but not >15%; `S=115` has no >15% warning; `S=116` warns >15%; `C=NULL,S=100` has null comparison and can still qualify. Warnings neither block confirmation/coverage nor require an extra acknowledgement checkpoint. True offer blockers are unresolved Supplier, cross-tenant/scope proof, required source/confirmation missing, corrupt/unavailable artifact, unresolved integrity/authenticity concern, invalid Decimal/currency/unit or exact mapping, material missing terms, effective/expiry ineligibility and absent required human confirmation. Missing ordinary draft facts yield INCOMPLETE preparation rather than fake successful confirmation. These rules never invoke APPRAISAL_RESULT comparison/finalization; that later policy's required comparison set/severity remains separately gated.

## 5. D10: conceptual commands, security, CAS, receipt and audit
