## F01–F04 reconciled requirements

### F01 — action-specific recovery

For a permissible working-price edit, retain official human Apply, fresh A7 validation and review, A8 Workbench reconfirmation, then one current independent full-set working confirmation. The operator must be able to reach each owning action while dependent NCCQ is stale. Existing proof, authorization, blocker handling and current-session/CAS validation continue to apply. A negative hold clears through its owning action.

Base supplier-quote readback and receipt access have their own scope. For supported withdraw/reject, derive a server capability for the particular action and target. Recover with current actor/grant/session, DRAFT status, policy/epoch, exact refreshed CAS/seal/full-line versions, target state and supplier/retained-source validity. A stale upstream state may hold register/confirm while the scoped withdraw/reject capability remains true. The UI must submit that valid target with fresh tokens even when top-level writable is false. Server checks remain authoritative. AC02, AC09, AC12 and AC22 require positive recovery, stale token/source and permission negatives, and confirmation that actual facts stay intact. Baseline UIT and SQAPI confirm/deny assertions are regression seeds.

### F03 — invocation, working and actual currentness

1. Invocation CAS detects a stale request. It can change after an unrelated edit or display-row revision.
2. Working proof binds the current exact seal, A7/A8 and human appraisal prices to selected independent source dependencies. A change can hold NCCQ until recovery while actual quote data and eligibility remain intact.
3. Quote-wide proof includes the authoritative current confirmed quote head, exact retained actual DocumentRevision and its authoritative custody/head, same-tenant/Project/document binding, immutable generation/hash/length, legal issuer identity/active/merge status, applicable terms/currency/deadline, receipt and concerns. A true shared fault holds all dependent items. A supplier display-field row version may affect invocation CAS without changing legal identity.
4. Item-local proof binds exact actual item/source locator, applicable terms and the semantic mapping of line identity/spec/unit/quantity and issuer/slot. Whole-seal or group revision changes belong in exact partition, slot completeness or invocation checks unless they genuinely change the particular item's dependency. Preserve unchanged item eligibility when the shared proof and mapping are still current.

Slot COMPLETE requires the full exact group in **one** current confirmed quote revision, with no duplicate line/item/source locator and no stitching across revisions. The confirmed quote head and the retained document head serve different proofs. Keep a confirmed head during negotiation only while current and its exact retained-document custody/head passes its owning rule. Never omit the document-head check or drop back to an older revision after withdrawal, correction, supersession or integrity failure. B04/B08 must settle custody during sent/return and negotiation. AC08–AC11, AC17 and AC25 require actual positive item-local assertions and negative shared/slot/head cases.

### Recovery proof: v1 reachable, v2 proof obligation remains

1. Saved draft alone does not change official value. Human apply writes P and increments row version (EDIT). `input_digest` changes even without membership change. A7 proof becomes stale; A8 content binding loses currentness. A10 upstream binding and A13 v1 dependencies hold.
2. `ValidateProjectAssetLine` requires DRAFT/current seal/lineage and current tokens, not A10/A13 COMPLETE. New generation computes rules; `DecideProjectAssetLineReview` requires valid proof, correct predecessor/reason and no mandatory blocker. Negative hold isn't cleared by mere validate.
3. `ConfirmProjectAssetWorkbench` requires A7 COMPLETE/current seal, all exact line tokens, valid descriptions, current predecessor. New confirmation allowed when content not current. No downstream quote prerequisite in inspected branch. A10 v1 must then re-establish fresh relationships and set confirm.
4. **Existing executable test definitions inspected (not run):** `test_human_commit_invalidates_confirmation_then_explicit_proofs_and_reconfirmation` (AWT:162–178, parameters description and price 42.50) asserts COMPLETE while draft, STALE after official apply, historical receipt replay, accept_all and workbench reconfirm COMPLETE. `test_official_content_writer_invalidates_and_recovery_requires_fresh_acceptance` (PET:223–238, price 52.50) asserts same then accept_all, workbench confirm, cover_all, price set confirm COMPLETE. This establishes a v1 recovery sequence in test code, not empirical run or v2 proof.
5. Target v2 graph is acyclic **if** the current writer/review capabilities remain available independent of stale downstream state and selected-working-proof commands depend only on structural readiness/current selected evidence. Then after A7/A8 recovery and one working set confirmation, quote prep can resume; actual quote eligibility calculated separately. No v1 qualifying acceptance command may be used to satisfy v2. Require integrated route/UI and PostgreSQL recovery test, including stale global writable, scoped withdrawals, concurrent edits, protected manual drafts, prior-negative hold, unchanged actual quote, no extra approval and receipt replays. If frontend or writer requires a later COMPLETE to repair earlier proof, STOP and reconcile. No removal of A7/A8 proof or hidden bypass. This is static conditional proof, not a claim that v2 is currently deadlock-free.

### 3. Currentness and lock proof obligations

Proposed proof domains follow [F03](#f03--invocation-working-and-actual-currentness). C_invocation pins policy epoch, scope, relevant versions and shared case token. C_work pins exact S, current A7/A8, selected independent source closure and admitted appraisal prices. Q_shared pins the current confirmed quotation head and exact retained document custody/head, actual issuer/terms/currency/expiry, receipt and holds. Q_item pins the particular semantic line/slot/issuer dependencies and exact item/locator/value. C_result pins work proof, current group-slot map, official result data and selected independent source references. A change to an unrelated semantic dependency or display metadata can change CAS alone. Compute actual item eligibility with both item and shared proof. Slot COMPLETE requires one current confirmed revision covering all exact lines with unique mappings and no stitching or fallback. Stage COMPLETE also checks current work, exact partition and absence of holds. Evaluate time on read and outer commit. Profile-dependent documents pin the subset actually used.

Candidate lock order for owning review: Project first, check active actor/org/grants/owned session and read mode/epoch under that lock. Reload exact seal/line versions and group/slot/quote heads in deterministic order. Protect shared identity/currency/document head and custody in compatible writer locks or equivalent commit-valid proof. Reserve receipt before append, flush fact and minimized audit inside the owning transaction, and recheck every affected target and deadline before outer commit. Lock graph, supplier-identity insertion, binary coexistence, all-writer participation and multi-target checks remain open under F02. Do not use a helper that independently commits part of the transition. Staged storage recovery follows its own authorized contract without deleting accepted bytes. Resolve unknown outcome by authorized historical lookup of the original receipt, then refresh the projection separately.

The policy resolver must agree across providers, commands, old binaries and receipt lookup. Admit explicit legacy v1 behavior for known cases only. Inconsistent mode/journal fails closed. Preview is read-only. Adopt exact proven existing actual references in an all-or-none epoch/journal commit and preserve the history. New v2 writes require a proven rollout barrier. Disablement after v2 writes may hold writes; v1 handling of that dossier remains blocked. Rehearse both writer orders and authorized forward repair on isolated synthetic PostgreSQL and retained storage after runtime authorization.
