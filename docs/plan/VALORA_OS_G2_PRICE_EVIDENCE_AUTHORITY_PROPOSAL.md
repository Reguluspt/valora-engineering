## 3. Business meaning and minimum v1 evidence model

The professional answers: **“What dated, inspectable source or reasoned basis supports considering a price for this prepared asset, and why is it relevant?”** ASSET_WORKBENCH prepares the asset; PRICE_EVIDENCE accepts its basis; SUPPLIER_QUOTES manages supplier-specific quotation/confirmation; SUPPLIER_SELECTION chooses a confirmed supplier quote; APPRAISAL_RESULT later records final professional price/result. No number in this stage means the latter three have occurred.

Use a small project-scoped evidence aggregate with immutable revisions and append-only decisions. The following are conceptual facts, not prescribed tables or a new generic evidence platform. Retain existing file/document identities where genuinely proved; supplement missing authority rather than replacing the evidence library or overloading QuoteLine.

| Fact | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence identity/revision | Stable evidence UUID, immutable revision UUID/number, organization and project IDs, canonical content digest, human registrar/time and predecessor identity. A record belongs to one project; reuse elsewhere requires its own explicitly admitted scoped record. |
| Source identity/revision | Project-scoped stable source UUID and immutable revision/digest; publisher/author/origin, source category, bounded reference and exact locator, source/effective date or explicitly recorded unknown date plus reason, observed/captured timestamp, and capture method. An optional legacy source ID is a locator, never ownership proof. No independent global mutable source catalogue is required. |
| Retained source material | Immutable bounded UTF-8 transcription/excerpt or authored explanation, preserving what the human actually registered. Record `manual_transcription` versus `authored_explanation`; never claim a transcription is authenticated original web bytes. A bare URL or amount is insufficient. Record limitations for human assessment. |
| Observed value facts | Source-stated amount or bounded range when present, decimal precision, currency, unit/quantity basis, tax/delivery/condition qualifiers and source locator. Explicit null/unknown distinctions; no implicit VND, FX conversion, tax assumption, float equality or automatic derivation of final unit price. A source without a value may support another item but cannot alone satisfy minimum price-basis coverage. |
| Unit-price explanation | Immutable authored method, dated rationale, identified inputs with source references and assumptions, calculations/units/currency and proposed basis value. A single manually typed price or circular reference to appraised_unit_price is not an explanation. Supporting input references must be retained and inspectable; mathematical consistency is checked deterministically, professional suitability remains human. |
| Historical appraisal source | Same-tenant prior dossier/project identity, exact historical asset, appraisal date, exact appraisal-result price/currency/unit, source document/revision or retained result excerpt and locator. Absence/unprovability of any required hop makes it ineligible; an old supplier quote cannot be relabeled. No unverified cross-tenant import or auto-copy. |
| Optional file/document reference | Typed evidence-file or document-revision ID, ownership proof, immutable checksum and locator (page/table/row/section); no object key or provider URL in public action/audit context. It counts only after §10 admission proof. A mutable ProjectFile, EvidenceLink or CurrentHead alone is insufficient. |
| Evidence-to-line relationship | Immutable relationship UUID/revision, exact project/seal/membership and line/staging correspondence, evidence revision, source locator/portion and human relevance rationale. Bind current official line input/proof digest at acceptance. No “all matching assets” or canonical-asset inference. |
| Human suitability decision | Append-only accepted/rejected decision for the exact relationship and evidence/source revision, reviewer/time, bounded reason/limitations and applicability as-of date. Accepted decision includes a mandatory `review_due_at` UTC deadline later than acceptance; any earlier source expiry is an upper bound. Rejection needs reason. No AI/system decision. |
| Set confirmation | Immutable whole-set confirmation, exact seal/set, upstream Workbench confirmation/content/proof identity, deterministic manifest of qualifying relationship/acceptance/source revisions, contract digest, actor/time and atomic receipt/audit. Also bind the current relevant decision/withdrawal generation so a known negative cannot be ignored. |
| Draft metadata/history | Unsaved/local drafts are presentation; optional saved drafts are non-authoritative, versioned and scoped. Labels/notes may change only within drafts. Registered facts are corrected by explicit immutable successor; withdrawals/supersessions retain prior bytes, references, reasons, decisions and receipts. No cleanup of evidence used by decisions. |

**D4 source scope:** preserve the existing priority, not three mandatory sources per line. Internet survey can be a human-registered dated excerpt with clear publisher/reference/value/qualifiers; no fetch is needed. Unit-price explanation requires the structured facts above. Prior-result evidence requires its complete historical lineage. Catalogue material may support the Internet survey or explanation if its actual origin qualifies; images/emails/attachments are carriers, not additional priority categories. AI output, supplier quotations and unsupported personal estimates cannot be promoted into a primary category by choosing a label. Knowledge suggestions need the same human registration and acceptance as any other candidate.

A lower-priority qualifying source may be used when higher-priority evidence is unavailable or unsuitable. Its acceptance must record which higher-priority category was considered and a bounded reason for not using it; “priority” is not a requirement to fetch the Internet or invent an unsuccessful search. The set-wide human checks those reasons. Minimum count is a product readiness floor, not a legal/professional claim of sufficient valuation evidence in every case.

## 4. Exact coverage and COMPLETE predicate

Let `S` be the exact non-empty authoritative sealed asset set from ADR 0048. Never shrink it to visible rows, selected rows, assets with prices or assets with available evidence. A missing/rejected line remains in `S`.

For each `l ∈ S`, define `Q(l)` as relationships that have all of: exact same-tenant/project/seal/line lineage; a retained qualifying source/value or explanation under §3; current human acceptance for that revision and current official line/proof facts; intact receipt/audit; no withdrawal/supersession, unresolved rejection, expiry or integrity failure. The source material is available through the controlled retained record. Duplicate links to the same evidence revision/portion count once.

Recommend `|Q(l)| ≥ 1` for every `l`, plus one set-wide confirmation. Additional evidence is allowed; count alone does not resolve contradictions. A known material contradiction/rejection relating to a proposed basis must be explicitly resolved/superseded by a human with reason, not hidden by registering a second positive item. Rejected optional candidates do not block forever: their immutable decision must explicitly classify them as excluded alternatives without an unresolved suitability concern. Unresolved suitability holds on a line block that line and the stage.

**Multi-line sharing:** one immutable source/evidence revision may support multiple sealed lines within the same project only through a separate relationship and explicit acceptance per line, each with an exact source portion, observed basis and line-specific relevance. No project attachment or batch selection grants universal coverage. A shared source revision/withdrawal affects every dependent relationship. Do not share mutable decisions across projects or tenants.

Partial registration/acceptance is retained while the stage stays INCOMPLETE. A project-level source without accepted line relationships has zero coverage. A whole-set confirmation is permitted only when all lines qualify and all holds are resolved. It attests the exact manifest, including the chosen current basis per line and disposition of considered alternatives; it does not select a final price. Any subsequent official evidence/relationship/decision change within that project requires a fresh set confirmation, even if minimum coverage remains satisfied; draft-only changes do not.

```text
PRICE_EVIDENCE COMPLETE iff
  accepted and implemented capability is available
  AND current upstream ASSET_WORKBENCH is COMPLETE
  AND S is exact, non-empty and lineage-intact
  AND every l in S has at least one qualifying current accepted relationship
  AND no higher-priority blocker or stale condition exists
  AND latest set confirmation is intact, non-withdrawn and current
      for exact upstream + evidence/decision manifest + accepted contract
  AND its command receipt and exactly-once success audit are intact.
```

No page visit, UI state, selection, draft, attachment presence, manual price, quote status, workflow Boolean or cached stage label is sufficient. Human confirmation is a durable input; the stage result remains read-derived, never a mutable `stage_done` flag.

## 5. Currentness, dates and invalidation

Use one canonical content serializer/digest aligned with upstream Decimal/null/string and lineage semantics. Evaluate only current active heads and their dependency closure: superseded/withdrawn historical versions stay inspectable but do not independently keep a repaired successor STALE. Negative holds require explicit human resolution and cannot be escaped by abandoning a record. PRICE_EVIDENCE intentionally uses STALE for obsolete evidence bindings; accepted ASSET_REVIEW still uses INCOMPLETE for ordinary input-proof revalidation. Tokens/row versions guard invocation; they are not the content proof. In particular, the confirmation's own Project increment must not invalidate itself.

| Change / condition | Proposed effect |
| --- | --- |
| Actual official line content change, including optional appraised_unit_price | Upstream validation/review and Workbench currentness retain accepted behavior. Prior evidence acceptance for that line and set confirmation become non-current; STALE diagnostic until fresh human acceptance and confirmation after upstream recovery. Same canonical-value writes do not invalidate merely by row version. |
| New validation generation, review supersession/reversal or Workbench confirmation generation | Old bound proof/confirmation identities no longer current, even if values/verdict match. Retain evidence material; re-evaluate acceptance and confirm the new set. Negative upstream holds remain blockers and are never cleared by evidence. |
| Extra/missing/replaced sealed member or tenant/line correspondence divergence | Proven divergence BLOCKED; corrupt/unprovable lineage STALE. No automatic membership repair, removal or rebinding. A later authorized membership lifecycle must explicitly invalidate dependent evidence. |
| Official evidence/source revision, correction, supersession or relationship change | No in-place edit. Prior acceptance/confirmation becomes non-current; a successor source is not followed automatically. Shared references invalidate all affected relationships. New registration also changes the set manifest; saved draft labels alone do not. |
| Evidence/relationship acceptance withdrawal | Append reversal; no coverage from it. A prior still-active set confirmation becomes STALE; without an active confirmation ordinary missing coverage is INCOMPLETE. Known unresolved negative suitability decisions take BLOCKED precedence. |
| Explicit set-confirmation withdrawal | With intact other facts, INCOMPLETE and history retained. No automatic rollback of evidence, working price or upstream acceptance. An independently stale source remains STALE. |
| Review deadline / source expiry | At server UTC time `now >= min(review_due_at, source_expiry if present)`, acceptance is non-current; STALE, even without a job writing status. A new human decision must reassess evidence; expired terms cannot be extended by changing a label. D8 proposes no universal 30/90-day financial rule or mandatory maximum evidence age. The human supplies a finite valid UTC deadline strictly after acceptance, with rationale; known source expiry is a hard upper bound. This consciously permits long-lived sources when justified; any later universal maximum is a separate PO policy decision, not a hidden runtime default. |
| Source date missing | Explicit unknown with reason may be accepted only if the human can substantiate temporal applicability and records a review deadline. Missing required historical appraisal date is ineligible. Unknown never silently becomes today. |
| External URL stops responding or changes | No background fetch/availability inference in v1. A retained dated source remains the historical source, not a claim of live availability. A newly human-recorded change/withdrawal invalidates dependent acceptance; controlled retained material becoming inaccessible/corrupt is STALE and cannot count. |
| Referenced source/document CurrentHead advances | No silent rebind. Historical immutable revision can remain the same dated source; an explicit supersession/withdrawal or broken pinned material invalidates it. Current live quotation authenticity is outside this stage. |
| Broken source/decision/receipt/audit chain or storage integrity unknown | STALE, never EMPTY or ordinary “not started”. No completion from metadata-only checksum assertions. Restore access/proof through authorized recovery, not SQL repair or replacement under the same ID. |
| Project leaves DRAFT | Content-current completion may remain COMPLETE. Unfinished work requiring an official mutation is BLOCKED outside DRAFT. No new registration/acceptance/confirmation/withdrawal permitted; no new return-to-DRAFT command. Status-only change is CAS/access, not evidence expiry. |
| Scoped BLOCKING issue opens/resolves | Preserve accepted scoped issue authority and precedence. Resolution can restore otherwise unchanged current completion; Warning is diagnostic, not a blocker. No issue CRUD authority added. |
| Permission/session changes; row/CAS change alone; page/layout/local draft/selection | Access and mutation eligibility may change; durable account-independent business facts do not. Stale client preconditions return conflict/reload, not a fabricated fresh STALE business result. |

Time-dependent eligibility must be evaluated once for a coherent read and again through the mutation commit boundary. The future shared token must include a deterministic freshness state/earliest relevant deadline so a previously issued “current” token cannot authorize confirmation after expiry; do not hash wall-clock time on every read. No cron/AI decision is needed to derive expiry.

Under accepted D10, all future new official commands use existing `workbench:edit`; this does not authorize their implementation. Reads use scoped `project:read`; session acquisition retains `workbench:open`. No session or permission becomes a completion predicate. All new writes are DRAFT-only, including reversals. 1.
